№ 02 · The Founder

From clothes to coffee, via the tropics.

Thongyoy Phaesuwan had run a clothing brand and three photography studios in Bangkok for nine years before she opened the cafe. The original plan was small — a corner cafe inside her fourth showroom, in her own Ari neighbourhood. It got out of hand. She invested a lot.

"Thai desserts, in my opinion, are undervalued because they are cheap. But speciality cafes across Thailand should not become something of a rarity." — Thongyoy Phaesuwan, Hive Life · Jul 2018

Joined by her sister and a friend, she fused her clothing-brand floral prints, an admiration of Thai culture and a dream of Madagascar into one room — wooden marble-topped tables, mini palm trees hovering overhead, hidden corners behind flower arches, and a kitchen that hand-picks the recipes for Thai sweets passed down over generations.

Two years later the second branch opened on the ground floor of Siam Paragon; a third followed at Central Ladprao. The platters stayed gold throughout. The Thai Dessert Set still costs ฿190. The Butterfly Pea Soda is still poured from a noodle-shop spice caddy.

№ 07 · Said in the press

From the fashion press to the food press.

Hive Life covered the founder transition; Time Out reviewed the room; TheSmartLocal made the cakes their cover. The chorus is consistent: gold platters, designer eye, traditional sweets done seriously.
