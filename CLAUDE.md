# THONGYOY CAFE (ทองย้อย คาเฟ่) — Bangkok

A regal, designer-led single-page tribute to THONGYOY CAFE — the Thai-dessert cafe that fashion designer Thongyoy Phaesuwan opened above her fourth clothing showroom in Ari, now expanded to Siam Paragon and Central Ladprao.

## Brand brief — verified

- **Name:** THONGYOY CAFE (ทองย้อย คาเฟ่). "Thongyoy" (ทองย้อย) = the Thai dessert "gold drops" — and the owner's own name.
- **Founder:** Thongyoy Phaesuwan — fashion designer, 32 in 2018, 9 years in fashion + 3 photography studios (Hive Life, Jul 2018)
- **Opened:** ~April/May 2018, Ari flagship
- **Concept:** "A cafe that fuses my passion for fashion, my admiration of Thai culture and my dream vacation in Madagascar" — Thongyoy Phaesuwan, Hive Life
- **Three locations:**
  1. **Ari (flagship)** 88/2 Soi Ari Samphan 7, Samsen Nai, Phaya Thai, BKK 10400. Mon–Fri 08–22 · Sat–Sun 09–22. Tel +66 82 030 6362.
  2. **Siam Paragon** G Floor, Pathum Wan, BKK 10330. Daily 10–22.
  3. **Central Ladprao** 4th Floor, Phahonyothin Rd, Chatuchak, BKK 10900. Daily 10–22.

## Verified menu items + prices

- **Thai Dessert Set** ฿190 — 5 assorted Thai desserts incl. Kleeb Lamduan (Thai shortbread), Luk Chup (mung-bean fruits), Khanom Tom (Thai coconut balls), ruby chestnuts in coconut milk
- **Bua Loi Cake** ฿150 — fusion based on rice-flour balls in sweet coconut milk
- **Passion Fruit Cheesecake** ฿150
- **Soda Set** ฿150 — two Thai sodas, served in a noodle-shop spice caddy
- **Butterfly Pea (Anchan) Soda** ฿65
- **Roselle (Kra-Jieb) Soda** ฿65
- **Coconut & toddy-palm cakes** (per Kirby's brief — signature; verified in IG product photos)
- **Gold-thread cheesecakes** (foi thong cheesecake — signature; verified in product photos)

Range: ฿75–฿190 (Hive Life founder interview + TheSmartLocal pricing).

## Brand voice (direct from founder, Hive Life Jul 2018)

> "Thai desserts, in my opinion, are undervalued because they are cheap. But speciality cafes across Thailand should not become something of a rarity."

> "I've literally searched everywhere in Bangkok and tasted all kinds of Thai desserts. I then selected the best of the best."

> "Many of the desserts here have recipes that have been passed down over generations."
